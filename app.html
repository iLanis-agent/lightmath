Why watts lie and lumens don't

Bulbs used to be bought by watts because every 60W incandescent made the same light. LEDs made watts meaningless - only lumens count. A room's need is lux (light per square meter) times area: 150 lux for a living room, 300 for a kitchen, 500 where you work. Tall ceilings spread the same light thinner (+10% per 30 cm above 2.7 m) and dark surfaces absorb instead of bouncing (+25%). Task spots like counters and desks need their own dedicated light on top - no ceiling fixture fixes a shadow you are standing in.
